# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Austin, TX homeowners (many in pre-1980 houses) with an urgent or planned plumbing need — a leak at 2 a.m., a failing water heater, a repipe decision. They arrive stressed or comparison-shopping, mostly from phones, and need to trust a contractor fast. Secondary audience: the business owner evaluating this as their web presence (the page is a portfolio-grade demo case).

## Product Purpose

A single-page conversion site for Westbrook Plumbing Co., a family-owned Austin plumber. Success = a phone call or a booked visit: every section funnels toward the phone number or the contact form. It must look equally credible on a phone at 2 a.m. and on any desktop monitor.

## Brand Personality

Honest, established, craftsman-warm. Three generations of one family; flat-rate pricing; "we walk if we can't do it for the quote." Editorial serif (Fraunces) + utilitarian sans (Instrument Sans) + mono accents (JetBrains Mono) over a bone/paper palette with midnight navy and brass.

## Anti-references

- Franchise-plumber template sites: stock smiling techs, red "CALL NOW" buttons everywhere, coupon pop-ups.
- Generic SaaS-landing gloss (gradient heroes, glassmorphism) — this is a trades business, not a startup.
- Fabricated-looking claims; all trust content stays specific and verifiable in tone.

## Design Principles

1. **The phone number is the product** — it stays huge, reachable, and clickable at every viewport.
2. **Proof over adjectives** — licenses, review counts, named people, written-quote promise carry the persuasion.
3. **One page, one funnel** — every section ends nearer to "call" or "book".
4. **Same site on every screen** — the desktop design scales proportionally with viewport width; no dead gutters on large monitors, no cramped overflow on small ones.
5. **Craftsman restraint** — brass hairlines, editorial type, muted photography; nothing shouts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA intent: visible focus rings, aria labels on interactive/decorative split, `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks (hero video hidden, transitions collapsed), 44px+ touch targets on mobile, semantic landmarks, live regions for form/ZIP feedback.
