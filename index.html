Common Questions

Questions before you call?

The seven things people ask most. If your question isn’t here, call — we answer live, twenty-four hours a day.

Answered live — 24/7
Do you actually answer at 2 a.m.?
+

Yes — real human, every call, every hour. We have a live dispatcher on overnight shift (it’s Maria — see Our People above). For emergencies, we dispatch within the hour to the Austin metro and most of Travis and Williamson counties.

How fast can you actually arrive?
+

For emergencies in Austin proper: typically 45–75 minutes from your call to our truck at your door. Cedar Park, Round Rock, Pflugerville: 60–90 minutes. We send an SMS with a 15-minute heads-up when we’re close, and we share live tracking from dispatch.

Will I know the price before you start working?
+

Always. We diagnose first, then write a flat-rate quote on paper before opening a tool case. You sign, we work. The price you sign is what you pay — even if the job runs long. If we can’t do it for the quoted price, we leave and you owe nothing.

Are you licensed and insured?
+

Texas Master Plumber license #MPL18492 (Miguel) plus three licensed journeymen on staff. $1,000,000 general liability and $500,000 workers’ compensation. All techs are background-checked and drug-tested. Documentation is shown with every quote — we can also email it ahead of your call.

What if I’m not happy with the work?
+

We come back at no charge until you are. Every job carries a 1-year workmanship warranty on top of the manufacturer warranty on any parts we install. Most water heaters carry 6–12 years through Rinnai, Rheem, and A.O. Smith.

Do you offer financing?
+

Yes, through Wisetack for jobs over $500. Soft credit check that doesn’t affect your score, fixed monthly payments, no prepayment penalty. Approval in about 60 seconds. We can run it before you sign — no obligation.

Where exactly do you serve?
+

Austin and surrounding cities — Cedar Park, Round Rock, Pflugerville, Leander, Buda, Kyle, Georgetown, Hutto, Lakeway, Bee Cave, West Lake Hills, and Sunset Valley. Use the ZIP-check tool above to confirm your address instantly.
